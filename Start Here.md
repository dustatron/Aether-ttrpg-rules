# The idea
This game is a bit of a thought experiment around magic items and where they originate from.
Why do powerful magic users make them, and why are they constantly leaving they just laying around every where?

Why do spells and magic items have such specific rules and limitations? 
It seems to me that magic, if it existed, would be way stranger, zany, and outcomes highly unpredictable. 
Like all forms of energy and power, the larger the source of energy, the greater the danger and cost to harness it. 
I think of nuclear energy and how amazing and almost endless that source of heat science can convert to electricity is but it leaves toxic waste behind and if not handled exactly right it would leave a wake of destruction that preeminently alters the surrounding area.
 
With that I imagine a source of magic called the Resonance that underpins all of existence and is one massive stream of energy that literally creates the reality we live in. 

So, lets play in that world, and allow the story to unfold without worrying about encounter balance or players becoming over powered.  I want a game where player progression is directly influenced by actions taken in the narrative and that each use of magic is balanced by its characters personal cost.

I like having a mix of rules sets from Power by the Apocalypse rules systems and Old School D&D inspired games like Shadowdark and OSE. Play to find out and the answer to your problems are in your imagination not your character sheet. 


## Key Terms
- **Resonance**: The energy that makes up all of reality.
- **Attuner**: A person capable of directly altering the Resonance.
- **Exposure**: The amount of Resonance an Attuner has absorbed. It increases their power but slowly destroys them.
- **Mutation**: A physical or mental change caused by Resonance Exposure.
- **Frayed**: A common folk term for an Attuner whose Exposure has begun to visibly alter them. 
- **Demon**: An Attuner who has been completely transformed by Resonance Exposure.

# THE WEAVE — Start Here

> [!abstract] A rules-light game of magic, corruption, and the price of power
> All of reality is held together by one energy: **the [[The Weave|Weave]].** A rare few reach into it and *insist* the world change — and it obeys. But every reach marks them. Bend reality and it bends you. Reach too far and you become **untethered** — lost, monstrous, hunted.
> You play a **weaver.** This is the story of your brief, incandescent life.

> [!warning] Working draft — v0.1, rules in flux
> The active game is the **rules-light build** (Corruption + Hope/Fear). An older **Mana** build is shelved in [[archive/the-weave-playbook|the archive]] and is **not canon.** Unresolved bits are collected under [[#Open questions]] below.

---

## The core loop in four lines

1. **Roll 2d6 + stat (or casting bonus)** vs. a difficulty. One **Hope** die, one **Fear** die. → [[Core Rules]]
2. **[[Casting & Corruption|Weave]] raw and succeed → +1 [[Casting & Corruption|Corruption]].** Power is doom in one number.
3. **Every 5 Corruption → [[Mutation|mutate]]:** stronger casting, a new [[Spell Tags|spell tag]], a permanent scar. **6th time → monster.**
4. **Bleed Corruption into [[Magic Items|items]]** to survive it. Cast raw and take the mark, or cast safe through what you made.

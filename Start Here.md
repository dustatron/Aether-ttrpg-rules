# The Arcane Aether

The Arcane Aether is a rules-light fantasy game about ordinary people who discover that reality can be rewritten.

Its inspired by Powered by the Appocylpes, Shadowdark, Blades in the Dark, Cairn RPG and other games that incurrage players to build their characters through play. 

I have two key ideas I am trying to explore with these rules. 
- Can magic be more open ended and weird while still being fun? 
- Can we have a simple system that implement character progression during play from the choices made inside a game session.

Over all this game doesn't concern itself with balance or really offering a ton of guard rails. 
I was hoping more to have simple game that is faster to learn, which to get into game play, and encourages silly, out of the box thinkings. I also like the idea that you could bring any magic from any other game you like and just see how it play in this game. 

As a GM I like the idea of play to find out and a free form play style. 
So I also wanted a simple system for monster creation and fluid flights, if thats how the players want to play. 

## Why This Game Exists

Why are there so many magic items? Why are the people capable of making them constantly leaving them in tombs, shops, and holes in the ground?

In this game, a magic item is somewhere an Arcanist put the power that was destroying them. Relics are not rewards dropped into the world by an invisible economy. They are evidence that someone survived long enough to leave part of their curse behind.

That was the idea I wanted to explore. The rest of the game grew around it.

## Why You Might Want to Run It

### Magic is a conversation, not a list

There is no spell list. A player says what they want their character's magic to do. You price its reach, force, and duration, then the player decides whether the effect is worth the Exposure.

You do not need to predict every possible spell. You only need to understand the situation and make the cost clear.

### Characters advance through their choices

Characters gain Luck when things go badly and when they complete goals they chose for themselves. Players spend that Luck to improve their characters, help one another, or reveal something prepared in a flashback.

Using magic also teaches new Spell Tags, but it fills the character's limited inventory with permanent Mutations. Progress and danger are tied to what happens during play.

### The game asks for very little preparation

A monster needs four numbers, a Weakness, and a Desire. That is enough to improvise a creature, give the players something to discover, and leave room for a bargain that is more interesting than another attack roll.

You do not need to balance every encounter. Tell the players what their characters can see, let them decide what risks to take, and follow the consequences.

### Strange ideas are allowed to work

Not everything needs a roll. If a character knows how to speak Orc because they once spent a summer in an Orc camp pretending to be a baby Orc's doll, that sounds good to me.

The character sheet provides pressure and useful boundaries. It does not contain the answer to every problem.

## What Play Looks Like

Most uncertain actions use **2d6 plus a Stat**:

- On 6 or less, the action fails and the player gains Luck.
- On 7 to 9, the character succeeds with a complication.
- On 10 or greater, the character succeeds fully.

Casting uses the same roll, but the player first accepts an Exposure cost. At 10 Exposure, their character Mutates and loses an inventory slot. Relics let Arcanists move Exposure into objects, but those objects also take space and eventually get left behind.

Combat uses simple zones, short Harm tracks, breakable Armor, and monsters built to fit on an index card. When Mutations, Scars, and Fatigue claim all twelve inventory slots, the condition that took the final slot determines the character's fate.

## Key Terms

- **Aether:** The particles and patterns that make up reality.
- **Arcanist:** A person who can perceive and command those patterns.
- **Exposure:** Raw Aether accumulated through casting.
- **Mutation:** A permanent change caused by Exposure.
- **Spell Tag:** Something the character's magic has learned to do reliably.
- **Relic:** An object that stores Exposure and performs one kind of magic.
- **Frayed:** A common term for an Arcanist visibly changed by Exposure.
- **Demon:** An Arcanist whose final inventory slot was claimed by a Mutation.

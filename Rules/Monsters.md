# Monsters

I want to be able to grab a creature from a monster book I already own and run it on the spot. Keep what makes the creature recognizable, including how it attacks. Four numbers and one question. That fits on an index card.

## Tiers

1. **Pick a tier that fits the encounter, or roll a six-sided die.** Copy Threat, Damage, Health, and Armor from that row.
2. **Give it a Desire.** What does it want besides killing the characters? That gives them something to bargain over.
3. **If it’s a [[#Mythic monsters|Mythic]], give it a Weakness.** For other tiers, add one if it makes the encounter more interesting.

| Tier level | Tier label   | Threat | Damage | Health | Armor | Example        |
| ---------- | ------------ | ------ | ------ | ------ | ----- | -------------- |
| 1          | Pest         | -1     | 1      | 3      | 0     | Goblin         |
| 2          | Minion       | 0      | 2      | 5      | 1     | Bandit         |
| 3          | Glass Cannon | +1     | 3      | 10     | 0     | Mummy          |
| 4          | Menace       | +2     | 3      | 15     | 2     | Bear or ooze   |
| 5          | Boss         | +2     | 4      | 20     | 3     | Demon          |
| 6          | Mythic       | +3     | 5      | 30     | 5     | Dragon         |

**Bandit, Minion**

**Threat** 0, **Damage** 2, **Health** 5, **Armor** 1

The bandit swings a club and wants a toll from anyone crossing the bridge.

If a monster has a Weakness, give the characters a way to discover it. They might find a corpse, read a book, or meet someone who faced the creature before.

For a monster caster, roll twice on [[Spell Tags|Spell Tag Sparks]] and combine the results into a casting theme.

More examples are in the [[#Bestiary Selection|Bestiary]].

## Running one

- **One attacker.** Roll 2d6 plus Threat. Use the monster’s listed Damage for any of its attacks. A bear might bite or swipe, but both deal 3 damage. See [[Core Mechanics#When enemies attack]].
- **Several attackers, one target.** When monsters attack one character at the same time, roll once using the group’s highest Threat and best single Damage. Their numbers can still surround characters, block exits, or create other complications.
- **Extra attacks.** A monster with Threat +2 or higher can attack twice on its turn. An individual monster may break this rule when it fits.

## Morale

When a monster falls to half Health or less, roll 2d6 plus Threat.

| Total | Result |
| --- | --- |
| 6 or less | It flees, surrenders, or bargains. |
| 7 to 9 | It changes tactics or pursues its Desire. |
| 10 or greater | It holds. |

The GM may call for another morale roll after the monster takes more damage. Mindless, driven, or bound creatures do not roll Morale.

## Mythic monsters

Health alone never kills a Mythic. Its Weakness is the only thing that can end it.

The characters might be able to hurt a dragon guarding his hoard in the mountain. To kill him, they have to exploit his Weakness.

When a Mythic reaches zero Health without its Weakness being exploited, it withdraws or changes. If it changes, restore its Health and make it worse.

Telegraph this from the beginning. Wounds close. Severed limbs reform. The blade comes away clean. Killing the figure beneath the robe only kills the robe.

## Bestiary Selection

### Goblin, Pest
#### Tier 1
**Threat:** -1 | **Damage:** 1 | **Health:** 3 | **Armor:** 0
- **Attacks:** Rusty knife, rocks, biting.
- **Weakness:** Bright light drives them back.
- **Desire:** Something shiny, and to see tomorrow.

### Mummy, Glass Cannon
#### Tier 3
**Threat:** +1 | **Damage:** 3 | **Health:** 10 | **Armor:** 0
- **Attacks:** Grasping hands, choking wrappings.
- **Weakness:** Fire.
- **Desire:** To punish whoever opened the door.

### Bear, Menace
#### Tier 4
**Threat:** +2 | **Damage:** 3 | **Health:** 15 | **Armor:** 2
- **Attacks:** Bite, swipe, bull rush.
- **Weakness:** Fire, which no animal will face.
- **Desire:** To be left alone.

### Ooze, Menace
#### Tier 4
**Threat:** +2 | **Damage:** 3 | **Health:** 15 | **Armor:** 2
- **Attacks:** Engulf, acid touch, drops from above.
- **Weakness:** Salt, or high ground.
- **Desire:** To dissolve and absorb metals.

### The Frayed Man, Boss
#### Tier 5
**Threat:** +2 | **Damage:** 4 | **Health:** 20 | **Armor:** 3
- **Attacks:** Grasping limbs, a crushing grip.
- **Casts:** Up to 8 Exposure. Bind, Drain, Mutate.
- **Weakness:** Hot gossip. He just cannot get enough.
- **Desire:** To kill Steve.

Too many joints, a second mouth, something moving under the coat.

### The Last Dragon, Mythic
#### Tier 6
**Threat:** +3 | **Damage:** 5 | **Health:** 30 | **Armor:** 5
- **Attacks:** Crushing jaws, sweeping tail, furnace breath.
- **Weakness:** It must be killed with something that has never been used as a weapon.
- **Desire:** To destroy every surviving account of dragons so no one remembers how to kill one.

Ancient, scarred, too large for the ruins it calls home, and remembers every wound.

# Thief

Lived by wit, speed, and other people's things.

| Strength | Dexterity | Willpower | Charm | Health |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| -1 | +2 | +1 | 0 | 6 |

## Gear

- Double daggers, 2 damage
- Leather Armor, Armor 1
- Lockpick kit
- Grappling hook
- 60 feet of rope
- Smoke pellets that hide the character from sight
- Rations x3

| d6  | Perk                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Super Taster: Your character can identify a substance by tasting even the smallest trace. Poison, strange ink, expensive perfume, someone's lunch. They can even tell what kind of creature left a smear of dried blood. Knowing what it is does not make it safe to taste. |
| 2   | Deal +1 damage to something that has not seen your character.                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| 3   | Roll with advantage when pickpocketing or lock-picking.                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| 4   | Vital Strike: When you roll double 4s while stabbing a creature unaware of your character's presence, the blade finds a vital spot. The creature dies before the blade is pulled free. Mythic creatures still require their Weakness to be exploited. |
| 5   | Call in a Favor: Once a session, you can declare that a thief, smuggler, or other shady person nearby owes your character a small favor. Neither is willing to explain why.                                                                           |
| 6   | Rat Whisperer: Your character can talk with rats and bargain with them to run errands, spy, collect information, or do other favors. Rats want food or favors in return, and their requests are not always reasonable. |

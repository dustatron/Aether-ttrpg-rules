# Performer

Your character learned to turn heads before they learned to earn an honest living. A street-corner singer, a dazzling circus dancer, or a hungry kid tumbling for coins. They know how to work a crowd, make an entrance, and turn a little trouble into a show.

| Strength | Dexterity | Willpower | Charm | Health |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| -1 | +1 | 0 | +2 | 6 |

## Gear

- Short sword, 2 damage
- Disguise kit
- A ring of stolen keys
- Padded coat, Armor 1
- Torch
- Rations x3

| d6  | Perk                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Once a session, name someone who owes your character a favor. They do.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| 2   | Musical Number: Once a day, your character can entice a roomful of ordinary people into an elaborate song and choreographed dance. Bakers, pickpockets, and visiting aunts know every word and step, and gleefully join in to perform the number. |
| 3   | Perfect Pitch: Your character can reproduce any voice, melody, or sound they have heard. A birdcall, a squeaky hinge, or the captain ordering the guards to stand down. |
| 4   | Kindred Spirits: When you roll double 4s on a Charm roll, your character and the target form a sudden, deep, and lasting bond. Neither can explain it, but both feel it. Resolve the roll normally.                                               |
| 5   | Throw Your Voice: Your character can make their voice sound as though it comes from anywhere nearby. A locked cupboard, someone else's mouth, or just behind the guard. |
| 6   | Master of Disguise: Your character is an expert with makeup, costumes, and accents, with an uncanny ability to convincingly pass as all sorts of people.                                                                                          |

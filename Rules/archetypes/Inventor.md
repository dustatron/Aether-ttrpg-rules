# Inventor

Your character learned to make machines and tools, think mad scientist or kooky professor. They built vehicles, traps, clockwork creatures, or a powerful weapon that should probably not have been created. 

| Strength | Dexterity | Willpower | Charm | Health |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 0 | +2 | +1 | -1 | 6 |

## Gear

- Hammer, 2 damage
- Tool roll with pliers, files, screwdrivers, and a hand drill
- A bundle of wire, springs, and spare parts, one inventory slot
- Thick leather apron, Armor 1
- Goggles
- Torch
- Rations x3

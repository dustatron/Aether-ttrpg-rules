# Inventor

Your character learned to make machines and tools, usually by taking apart something that was working perfectly well. They built pumps, traps, clockwork toys, or a kettle that could follow its owner around. Awakening gave them a whole new way to make things go wrong. They still carry a hammer. Some problems respond well to a hammer.

## Gear

- Hammer, 2 damage
- Tool roll with pliers, files, screwdrivers, and a hand drill
- A bundle of wire, springs, and spare parts, one inventory slot
- Thick leather apron, Armor 1
- Goggles
- Torch
- Rations x3

| d6 | Perk |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Field Repairs: With tools and ten quiet minutes, your character can repair one broken piece of manufactured Armor or a shield, restoring its original Armor value. |
| 2 | Just the Thing: Once per session, declare that the character's tool roll contains a small, ordinary tool needed for the current problem. A glass cutter, a tuning fork, or a very particular screwdriver. It stays part of the tool roll and takes no extra slot. |
| 3 | Working Prototype: Agree with the GM on a small, nonmagical device that performs one simple job. Spend 1 Luck, and the character can build it with tools, suitable scraps, and ten quiet minutes. A door alarm, a grabber for something down a drain, or a winch to raise a stuck hatch. It takes one inventory slot and works for one scene after its first use, then breaks. |
| 4 | Spanner in the Works: When you roll double 4s on a melee attack, your character may jam, pin, or tangle one nonmagical weapon the target is holding. It cannot be used until someone spends an action freeing it. Resolve damage and complications normally. |
| 5 | See How It Works: Roll with advantage when your character examines a machine or mechanical trap to work out how it operates or how to disable it. |
| 6 | Clockwork Helper: Your character has built a little mechanical creature. Choose its shape. It occupies one inventory slot even while active. It can move within Near range of the character and carry an object small enough to fit in one hand. In a turn, spend the character's action to give it one simple task, which it attempts immediately. For an uncertain task, roll with the character's Dexterity. It cannot attack. A damaging hit wrecks it; an hour with tools and suitable scraps rebuilds it. |

# Monk

Your character practices ancient martial arts, leaping between rooftops, balancing on swaying branches, and meeting drawn steel with empty hands. Their body is a weapon, their mind just as sharp. They might catch a bullet with their teeth or meditate in the heart of a storm.

| Strength | Dexterity | Willpower | Charm | Health |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| +1 | +2 | 0 | -1 | 7 |

## Gear

- Walking staff, 2 damage
- Bandages and salves, 3 uses, restore 1d6 Health
- 60 feet of knotted rope
- Throwing stars x4
- Torch
- Rations x3

| 2   | Meeting Steel: Spend 1 Luck when a melee attack would hit your character. They turn the blow aside, taking no damage, or redirect it into another target within the attack's reach. |
| 3   | Light as a Leaf: Your character can leap 20 feet and balance on branches, blades, or slender objects that should never support their weight. A foothold no wider than a toe is enough. They can fight while perched there. |

| 5   | A Most Humble Demonstration: Your character can split a brick, catch a fly with chopsticks, or balance a full teapot on one finger. Ordinary displays of precision and discipline need no roll. Doing them while someone tries to kill them is another matter. |

# Wizard

Your character studied ancient magic, copied books older than their school, and learned to operate relics whose makers were long dead. They may have taught apprentices, advised someone important, or been thrown out over an argument about footnotes. Then an Aetherborn awakened them. A great deal of their education suddenly needed revising.

## Gear

- An old teaching wand, a relic holding 9 Exposure. Choose its one magical function with the GM. It follows the [[Magic System#Magic Items and Dispersing Exposure|normal relic rules]].
- Walking staff, 2 damage
- A book of lecture notes, diagrams, and corrections to other people's diagrams
- Ink, quills, and chalk
- Torch
- Rations x3

| d6 | Perk |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Dead Languages: Your character can read any written language, including languages no living person speaks. Codes and missing passages still need to be worked out. |
| 2 | Relic Appraiser: After your character spends an undisturbed minute examining a relic, the GM tells you its magical function, how to activate it, and how much Exposure it holds. Examining it does not activate it. |
| 3 | I Read About This: Once per session, play a free flashback to your character researching something relevant to the present situation. Perhaps they consulted a forbidden book or argued with its author. Resolve the scene using the normal [[Core Mechanics#Flashbacks\|flashback rules]]. |
| 4 | Careful Casting: When the kept casting dice are double 4s, choose one creature or object within the spell's area to leave unaffected. Choose before resolving the effect, then resolve the cast and any complications normally. |
| 5 | Academic Authority: Roll with advantage when your character uses their scholarly reputation to persuade someone to grant access to a library, collection, or learned society. |
| 6 | Patient Study: Once per day, after your character spends a quiet hour studying their notes, fill one segment of an unfinished Spell Tag that fits a successful cast they made that day. This can complete the Tag at +1. |

# Wizard

Your character studied the ancient scrolls, copied books older than their school, and learned to operate relics whose makers were long dead. They may have taught apprentices, advised someone important, or been thrown out over an argument about footnotes.

- A Dagger, 2 damage
- A pipe
- A Lantern

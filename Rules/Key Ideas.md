# Key Ideas

These ideas shape the game even when the rules do not say them outright.

## Key Terms

- **Aether:** The particles and patterns that make up reality.
- **Arcanist:** A person who can perceive and command those patterns.
- **Exposure:** Raw Aether accumulated through casting.
- **Magic Magnitude:** The overall scale of a spell, setting its Exposure cost. See [[Casting Magic#Magic Magnitude]].
- **Mutation:** A permanent change caused by Exposure.
- **Fatigue:** Exhaustion caused by going without proper rest or food, or consuming poison. Each Fatigue occupies one inventory slot.
- **Spell Tag:** Something the character's magic has learned to do reliably.
- **Health:** How much damage a character or monster can take before suffering worse consequences.
- **Relic:** An object that stores Exposure and performs one kind of magic.
- **Frayed:** A common term for an Arcanist visibly changed by Exposure.
- **Demon:** An Arcanist whose final inventory slot was claimed by a Mutation.

## Making money

Arcanists can make and sell relics. For some, it is a living.

Wizarding schools may keep their Arcanist suppliers on the down-low. Common folk do not need to know that their magic items come from people slowly turning into monsters.

## Magic is a team sport

Magic can be a team effort. Separate casts can build on each other.

One character creates fire, another opens a portal, and a third blows the fire through it. Price and roll each cast separately. Each character gains Exposure for their own cast.

## Arcanists might be scary

Anyone who can rewrite reality is dangerous. Common folk may fear Arcanists. Powerful people may hunt, recruit, hide, or control them before they grow into demigods.

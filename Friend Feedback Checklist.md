# Friend Feedback Checklist

This checklist separates reactions, questions, possible changes, and things that need playtesting. Feedback is not a rule change until a decision is recorded.

## Confirmed or working as intended

- [x] **Keep Otherfolk open-ended.** It gives creative players room without burdening players who want a familiar choice.
- [x] **Casting uses a Spell Tag instead of a Stat.** This is stated in [[Core Mechanics#The dice|The dice]] and [[Magic System#Magical casting|Magical casting]].
- [x] **The casting dials support different effects at the same price.** A touched target shoved for a few hours costs 3 Exposure. A room shoved for an instant also costs 3.
- [x] **Relics create a reason to move Exposure into objects.** The core idea landed well.

## Tragic failures and critical successes

- [x] Rename **Critical failure** to **Tragic failure**.
- [ ] Decide whether Tragic failures should be a group tone setting instead of required in every campaign.
- [ ] Decide whether double 1s in a volatile game override the total or add Wild Magic while the total resolves normally.
- [ ] Decide how the Wild Magic result escalates after each new double 1.
- [ ] Decide whether escalation resets each session or continues through the campaign.
- [ ] Playtest doubles with players who enjoy severe consequences and players who resist them.

### Proposed group toggle

Choose one before play:

- **Volatile Aether:** Double 1s trigger a roll on the Wild Magic table. Each new double 1 makes later Wild Magic rolls more dangerous.
- **Gentle Aether:** Double 1s do not cause a Tragic failure. Resolve the total normally and gain 2 Luck.

One possible escalation method is to roll one additional d66 for every Tragic failure already rolled and keep the highest result.

### Useful probability

- Double 1s on a normal 2d6 roll occurs **1 time in 36**.
- When rolling 3d6 and keeping the highest two, double 1s requires all three dice to show 1. It occurs **1 time in 216**.
- More rolls make the event likely to appear during play, even though any single roll is unlikely.

## Luck and flashbacks

- [ ] Watch whether players save all Luck for 5-point advancements instead of spending 2 Luck on flashbacks.
- [x] Pause the idea that spending Luck also earns XP.
- [x] Keep direct advancement at 5 Luck for the current playtest.
- [ ] Test this in both campaign play and one-shots.

### Paused idea: spending Luck earns XP

Whenever a player spends 1 Luck, they mark 1 XP. A player may spend Luck on another player's behalf and mark the XP themselves. Luck is spent immediately rather than passed back and forth.

At 5 XP, choose an advancement and reset XP to 0. If this rule is adopted, remove the option to spend 5 Luck directly on advancement.

This adds a small XP track, but it makes using Luck during play the path to advancement. Flashbacks, assistance, and improved rolls all move the character forward.

## Casting costs

- [x] The player calculates their character's Exposure cost and the GM confirms or adjusts it.
- [x] For scrying and other information magic, Reach measures the distance to the subject and Duration measures the observation time. Force and Effect is 0 when the spell only gathers information.
- [x] **Self** has the same Reach cost as touch.
- [ ] Decide whether one creature remaining invisible for up to a month should cost only 3 Exposure.
- [x] Keep equal Exposure costs for magical damage and healing for now. They are intended as opposing sides of the same effect.
- [ ] Playtest whether equal pricing favors the side with more casters.
- [x] Damage and healing normally happen instantly. A lasting healing spell creates a zone priced by Reach or Size, Force and Effect, and Duration. Each character can benefit once per cast.
- [ ] Test the casting dials with players who follow the spirit of the rule and players who deliberately search for extreme combinations.

### Current examples

- Touched creature + firm shove + a few hours = **3 Exposure**.
- Medium room + firm shove + instant = **3 Exposure**.
- One touched creature + invisibility + up to a month = **3 Exposure**.

## Overcasting

- [x] Remove Tolerance and casting-based Fatigue after playtesting them.

Current rule: successful casts mark their full Exposure. At 10 Exposure, the character gains a Mutation. Fatigue comes from missing proper rest or food, or consuming poison, not from casting.

## Inventory

- [ ] Test whether the 12-slot inventory creates enjoyable pressure or tedious tracking.
- [ ] Try it with physical inventory tiles as well as a written character sheet.
- [ ] Ask players whether dropping displaced items creates interesting choices or bookkeeping.

## Relics and Exposure storage

- [x] Limit each Arcanist to creating or refilling one relic per in-game day.
- [x] Use the daily limit instead of adding a creation roll, Fatigue, or another resource.
- [x] Allow inert Exposure containers, such as phylacteries or soul jars. They follow the normal relic rules but have no magical function.
- [x] Stored Exposure is not connected to the Arcanist who placed it there. Avoid tracking objects outside the character's inventory.
- [x] Characters may make and sell relics. This is an intended part of the economy and setting.

The current relic wording in the vault correctly subtracts an activation's Exposure cost from the relic. The rules PDF needs the same correction.

## Health and combat recovery

- [x] Rename the resource from **Harm** to **Health**.
- [x] Catching your breath is a Willpower action instead of guaranteed 1d6 recovery.
- [ ] Playtest catching your breath against magical damage and a couple of quiet hours.

### Current numbers

- Catching your breath restores **0, 1, or 2 Health** on a normal result. Double 6s restore 3 Health and give advantage on the character's first roll next turn.
- Magical damage costs Exposure and deals at most **3 damage** per cast.
- A couple of quiet hours restores **2d6 Health**, averaging 7.

## Idea bank

These ideas are collected for later. None are current rules.

### Luck and advancement

- Use physical dice as Luck. When spent, move them from the Luck pool to XP.
- Mark gained Luck with one slash, turn it into an X when spent, and clear completed marks after advancement.
- Let players spend Luck directly on another player's behalf. The player who spends it earns the XP.
- Make flashbacks available when a player holds 2 Luck instead of charging 2 Luck.

### Doubles

- Let each group choose between Volatile Aether and Gentle Aether.
- Let repeated double 1s escalate by rolling additional d66 results and keeping the highest.
- Allow a costly Luck payment to escape a Tragic failure, including Luck contributed by other players.

### Casting

- Let separate casts combine naturally. One character sprays oil, another creates fire, and a third shoves an enemy into it.

### Relics

- Explore magical schools hiding that their magic comes from people slowly becoming monsters.

### Health and recovery

- Playtest the new Willpower action for catching your breath.

## Magic Magnitude follow-up

- [ ] Proposed general reward: gain 2 Luck after resolving a consequential roll with GM-imposed disadvantage, whether it succeeds or fails. The GM decides whether it qualifies; trivial manufactured disadvantage does not. Decide whether canceled disadvantage qualifies and confirm whether failure Luck stacks. At 5 Luck per advancement, this could substantially accelerate progression.
- [ ] Playtest Mythic resurrection: Mythic magnitude, 10 Exposure, the caster always mutates, and the spirit must still be present, not moved on.
- [ ] Confirm whether the listed magical damage is a normal guideline or a ceiling. It is currently a guideline, agreed before rolling.
- [ ] Full-success casting now doubles standard spell damage. Decide whether casting double 6s should also triple damage; its current specific result still grants Spell Tag growth.
- [ ] Playtest the provisional Huge benchmark covering both a field and a city. The GM judges the whole effect, rather than treating every effect over the same area as equally demanding.
- [ ] Decide how many characters one healing cast can cover. The player picks the magnitude based on how many characters they heal, but no guideline says what each magnitude covers.

## Playtest

- [ ] Compare starting Unchanged with starting Marked. The Marked character gains a Mutation and a +1 Spell Tag, but begins with one fewer inventory slot.
- [ ] Test whether Fatigue from missed rest, missed meals, and poison creates useful pressure, and whether one full night's sleep should remove all Fatigue.
- [ ] Test flexible relic activations. A relic has one function, but the user can spend more stored Exposure for a greater Magic Magnitude.
- [ ] Confirm that a full-success casting flourish feels useful without increasing the agreed effect.
- [ ] Playtest ghost possession. The ghost rolls to take over a living body or object; on a success a possessed person still acts for themselves while the ghost can spend their action once per scene.
- [ ] Playtest the monster caster tier cap, and the visible permanent mutation that comes with reaching beyond it.

## Suggested order

1. Player-priced casting and casting-cost edge cases.
2. Luck spent on flashbacks versus advancement.
3. Tragic failures and consecutive doubles.
4. Inventory at the table.

# THE WEAVE — Making a Weaver
### Fast character creation. Build a person, then let the Weave find them.

*Aim for five minutes. You're making a human being first and a weaver second — the doom comes last, and it comes for everyone.*

---

## Step 1 — The Person

Write down the basics:
- **Name**
- **Age**
- **Pronouns**
- **Description** — a sentence on how they look and carry themselves.

---

## Step 2 — Life Type

What kind of folk are you? Each grants **one small trait.**

| Life Type | Trait |
|---|---|
| **Human** | *Adaptable.* Once per day, treat any one roll as if a spell tag applied (**+1**). |
| **Elf** | *Long-sighted.* +1 when you act with patience, precision, or old knowledge. |
| **Goblin** | *Overlooked.* +1 to hide, squeeze, scurry, or go unnoticed. |
| **Beastfolk** | *Clawed & keen.* You have natural weapons (**1 harm**) and sharp senses — +1 to notice by scent or sound, or to climb and leap. |

---

## Step 3 — Archetype

Who were you *before* the Weave? Your archetype is your old life — it suggests a **stat array**, gives you **starting gear**, and a single **perk.** (You can rearrange the array; it's a starting point, not a cage.) Archetypes are mundane — your magic comes later, from the Weave.

Everyone also carries a **torch, flint & steel, a bedroll, and rations.** Your archetype adds the rest.

**Fighter** — *you lived by strength and steel.*
- Suggested array: **Force +2, Grace +1, Wits 0, Heart −1**
- Gear: Longsword (2 harm), hand axe you can throw (1 harm), wooden shield (armor 1), chainmail (armor 1), a war-horn that carries for miles.
- Perk — *Hard to Put Down:* the first time you'd lose a life in a fight, stay up with 1 Heart instead.

**Thief** — *you lived by wit, speed, and other people's things.*
- Suggested array: **Grace +2, Wits +1, Heart 0, Force −1**
- Gear: Double daggers (2 harm), leather armor (armor 1), lockpick kit, grappling hook, 60' rope, smoke pellets (drop one to vanish from sight).
- Perk — *Slip Away:* Once per day, escape a grab, a corner, or a closing door without a roll.

**Scholar** — *you lived by books, signs, and things best left unread.*
- Suggested array: **Wits +2, Heart +1, Grace 0, Force −1**
- Gear: Quarterstaff (1 harm), spyglass, 3 reference books, a vial of poison, a magic stone that always returns to your hand.
- Perk — *I've Read About This:* Once per day, recall a small piece of trivial knowledge about something — or someone — you're investigating in the scene.

**Charmer** — *you lived by fast talk and knowing everyone worth knowing.*
- Suggested array: **Heart +2, Grace +1, Wits 0, Force −1**
- Gear: Hidden dagger (1 harm), padded coat (armor 1), disguise kit, marked cards & loaded dice, a ring that lets you recall any face you've seen.
- Perk — *Everyone Knows Me:* Once per day, you turn out to have history with someone present — you've done business, shared a drink, or run a scam together.

---

## Step 4 — Set Your Stats

Arrange **+2, +1, 0, −1** across your four stats:
- **Force** — strength, violence, enduring.
- **Grace** — speed, stealth, precision.
- **Wits** — perceive, know, scheme.
- **Heart** — charm, will, presence. *(The Weave erodes this as you mutate.)*

Use your archetype's suggestion or rearrange to taste. Everyone has one thing they're bad at — the −1 is a feature.

---

## Step 5 — Goals

**What does this character seek?** One or two things they want — a person to find, a debt to pay, a place to reach, a wrong to right. These are your reasons to act, and your reasons to keep using magic even as it damns you.

---

## Step 6 — Flaw or Fear

Name **one flaw or fear** in a sentence — *terrified of deep water · can't back down from a dare · lies when cornered · won't abandon anyone, ever.*

It's a piece of who your character is — the kind of thing that makes them feel real and shapes how they act.

> **Play it, gain Hope.** When you lean into your flaw or fear in the story — let it drive a choice, complicate a moment, or steer how you act — mark **1 Hope.** Playing your character honestly is its own reward.

---

## Step 7 — Backstory / Core Concept

A sentence or two tying it together — who you were, where you come from, what shaped you. Just enough to play. The rest you'll discover at the table.

---

## Step 8 — …Then You Were Exposed

Only those who've touched the raw Weave can ask it for favors. **How were you exposed, and learned to bend reality?** What was the incident that made you a weaver?

Pick one, or invent your own:
- You were **caught in another weaver's magic** and it soaked into you instead of killing you.
- You **survived a wild magic event** and walked out changed.
- A weaver **poured it into you on purpose** (and you may owe them for it).

*(Easy group option: one event exposed you all — same disaster, same survivors, instant bond and a shared mystery.)*

**Your first spell** — as the Weave took hold, something broke out of you: the first weave you ever cast, the one that revealed what you'd become. Name it. This is your **starting spell tag** (Flame, The Drowned, Rot, Binding…). It's not a style you chose — it's what surfaced when the Weave changed you.

---

## Step 9 — Starting Values

Fill in your sheet:
- **Corruption** 0
- **Hearts** 5
- **Hope** 0
- **Wounds** 0 *(death at 3 by default — the GM sets this)*
- **Mutations** 0
- **Spell tags** — your one first spell
- **Casting bonus** = mutations + a fitting tag (so **+1** in your first-spell style, **+0** otherwise)

You're a fresh weaver: shaky, wild, dangerous, and one bad roll from wonders or ruin. Go find out what you become.

---

## The Whole Thing at a Glance
1. Name, age, pronouns, description
2. Life Type (one trait)
3. Archetype (array + gear + perk)
4. Set stats: +2 / +1 / 0 / −1
5. Goals — what you seek
6. Flaw or fear — lean in, gain Hope
7. Backstory / core concept
8. Exposure + first spell (your starting tag)
9. Starting values: 0 Corruption · 5 Hearts · 0 Hope · 0 Wounds · 1 tag

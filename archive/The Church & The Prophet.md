# The Church and the Prophet

## The Church

The Church's temples stand openly in cities and villages. Its priests preach connection, obedience, and peace. Its followers believe they serve a benevolent prophet.

## The Prophet

At the center of the Church is an ancient Arcanist, perhaps the most Mutated being alive. He should have become a Demon long ago, but somehow remains in control of his form.

He seeks immortality and absolute control of the Aether.

## The Relics

The Prophet transfers Exposure into objects, creating powerful relics. He cannot supply the world's magical orders alone.

The Church secretly maintains Arcanists who produce relics for it. Their work feeds an underground trade that supplies the established orders with magic they cannot create themselves.

The Church hunts independent Arcanists who threaten that monopoly.

## The Church's Secret

- The Prophet's Mutations are hidden beneath robes, masks, and ritual.
- His monstrous nature has been transformed into something sacred.
- The institution exists to protect him, feed him, and prepare him for ascension.
- Almost no one knows his true name or his past.

# The Arcane Aether

## The hidden nature of reality

All of reality is made of countless particles called Aether. Matter, life, thought, space, and time are patterns formed from it.

Most people cannot perceive those patterns. Aetherborn can. Through thought and imagination, they command Aether to alter matter, bend natural laws, and create impossible effects. This is True Casting.

Every Aetherborn must be awakened by another Aetherborn. See [[Becoming an Aetherborn]] for Awakening and the chain of true casters stretching back through generations.

## The cost

Every cast exposes the Aetherborn to raw Aether. Exposure makes greater magic possible while destabilizing the Aetherborn's own pattern. As it builds, the Aetherborn Mutates. Too many Mutations turn them into a Demon. See [[Magic System#Exposure and Mutation|Exposure and Mutation]].

An Aetherborn can delay this transformation by moving Exposure into an object. The object becomes a relic that stores Aether and performs one kind of magic.

## The magical orders

The world has wizards, warlocks, witches, schools, spellbooks, potions, and enchanted objects.

Most conventional magic users do not command Aether directly. They are fancy scavengers using relics and knowledge left behind by Aetherborn.

The oldest magical orders know where their power comes from. Some trade protection, knowledge, and resources for new relics. Others hunt independent Aetherborn while quietly depending on their work.

Most people do not think Aetherborn are real. If they have heard the name at all, it belongs to an old myth, something vaguely connected to demons. They know little about what an Aetherborn actually is.

A visibly changed Aetherborn may be mistaken for a demon or some other monster. Those familiar with Aetherborn often call them Frayed.

## Making money

Aetherborn can make and sell relics. For some, it is a living.

Wizarding schools may keep their Aetherborn suppliers on the down-low. Common folk do not need to know that their magic items come from people slowly turning into monsters.

## Aetherborn might be scary

Anyone who can rewrite reality is dangerous. Common folk may fear what an Aetherborn can do without knowing what they are. Powerful people who know Aetherborn are real may hunt, recruit, hide, or control them before they grow into demigods.

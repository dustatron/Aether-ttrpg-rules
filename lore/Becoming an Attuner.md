# Becoming an Attuner

No one is born an Attuner. There are two ways to become one.

## Initiation: deliberate

An Attuner can deliberately expose another person to the Resonance, opening their mind to patterns most people cannot perceive.

## Exposure: accidental

A person can become an Attuner through accidental exposure to the Resonance. Survive enough direct contact and the Resonance may leave you changed, allowing you to perceive and manipulate its patterns.

This can happen after surviving a lethal use of Resonance, a wild magic event, or another uncontrolled encounter with raw Resonance.

No one chose to make you. You owe no one.

But an untrained Attuner is dangerous. Without guidance, they are more likely to become Frayed, and the magical orders have every reason to find them first.

## Every Attuner is a risk

Resonance exposure can create new Attuners.

A failed attempt to kill someone with the Resonance may awaken their ability instead. A wild magic event may expose everyone caught within it.

Every Attuner is a potential source of magic, a potential Frayed, and a potential threat to the established magical orders.

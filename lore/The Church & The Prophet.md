# The Church & The Prophet

> [!abstract] The center of the world
> Somewhere sits a weaver who has solved the equation the rest die to.

He is, by now, almost certainly the **most [[Mutation|mutated]] being alive** — barely human beneath the robes. By every rule he should have been lost to doom long ago, and hunted on sight. He is neither, because he built an institution to make both impossible.

## The Church is his funnel

A faith, a hierarchy, a stream of devoted ordinary people — and every one a candidate for [[Becoming a Weaver|initiation]]. He opens them to the Weave and taxes them for their life, harvesting their lifespan to extend his own.

> [!danger] The species is being strip-mined into one man
> His followers mutate, burn out, and are hunted; he endures on their accumulated life. **He is the reason weavers are vanishing.**

## The item trade is his valve, fortune, and disguise

Carrying a doom-sized reserve of power, he constantly bleeds it into [[Magic Items|objects]] — staving off the [[Mutation|mutation]] ledge, growing rich, and flooding the world with relics. Those relics are what the world's "wizards" and "witches" scavenge and call magic.

> [!quote] He is the secret source of nearly all magic in the world — and almost no one knows his name.

His monstrousness, which should mark him for the hunters, is reframed by the Church as **holy** — the sacred disfigurement of a prophet, hidden behind hood and mask and doctrine. The institution is camouflage for the monster.

## His goal

He seeks to become a **god** — to rule his followers not as prophet but as deity, and to seize and **control the Weave itself.**

This is the thing every other weaver's death warns against: he intends to reach the **6th [[Mutation|mutation]] and beyond** and *not* dissolve into an untethered demon — to walk through the doorway that unmakes everyone else and emerge still himself, enthroned. Whether the Weave *can* be ruled, or whether the attempt simply ends the world, not even he knows.

## Player hooks

> [!example] The harvested
> The players are his initiates — in debt, burning out to feed him. The campaign is escape from the pyramid, and that means cutting the funnel at its source.

> [!example] The wild
> The players are [[Becoming a Weaver|exposure-born]], unindebted, free — and the Church hunts them as both rivals and unclaimed vitality.

> [!example] The schism
> The players are highly placed within the Church and begin to see what the prophet truly is, and what his ascension will cost the world.

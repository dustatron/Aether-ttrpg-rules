# The Church & The Prophet

## The Church
The Church's temples stand openly in cities and villages. Its priests preach connection, obedience, and peace. Its followers are ordinary people who believe they serve a benevolent prophet.

## The Prophet
At the center of the Church is an ancient Attuner, perhaps the most mutated being alive. He should have become a Demon long ago, but somehow remains in control of his form.

He seeks immortality and absolute control of the Resonance.

## The Relics
The Prophet constantly transfers his excess Exposure into objects, creating powerful relics. But he cannot supply the world's magical orders alone.

The Church secretly maintains an army of Attuners who produce relics for the Church. Their work feeds an underground magic item trade that supplies the established magical orders with artifacts they could never produce themselves.

The Church uses this trade to build wealth and control. It also hunts independent Attuners who create and sell relics outside the Church's network. No rival source of Resonance magic is allowed to threaten its monopoly.

The relic trade funds the Church, helps manage the Prophet's Exposure, and keeps the magical orders dependent on him.

## The Church's Secret
- The prophets mutations are hidden beneath robes, masks, and ritual.
- The prophets monstrous nature has been transformed into something sacred.
- The institution exists to protect him, feed him, and prepare him for his ascension.
- Almost no one knows his true name or his past.

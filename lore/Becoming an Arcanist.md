# Becoming an Arcanist

No one is born an Arcanist. They glimpse the hidden patterns of Aether and return able to command them.

## Deliberate Exposure

An Arcanist may deliberately expose another person to raw Aether. If the person survives, their mind may open to patterns most people cannot perceive.

## Accidental Exposure

Someone may awaken after surviving a magical disaster, a Wild Magic event, or another uncontrolled encounter with raw Aether.

No one chose to make them. They owe no one.

## What the Exposure Leaves Behind

During [[Character Creation]], the player decides whether their character starts Unchanged or Marked.

An Unchanged character can perceive and command Aether, but has not yet Mutated. A Marked character begins with one Mutation and a related +1 Spell Tag.

Every Arcanist is a source of new magic, a possible creator of relics, and a possible future Demon. The magical orders have reasons to find them first.

# The Weave

> [!abstract] The premise
> All of reality is held together by a single source of energy: **the Weave.** It is the fabric and the loom both — every stone, every breath, every law of nature is the Weave holding its shape.

A rare few gain access to it. They are **weavers.** A weaver does not learn spells. A weaver reaches into the fabric of the world with their mind and *insists* it change — and the world, sometimes, obeys. (See [[Casting & Corruption|Casting]].)

The cost is written into the act. Touching the Weave touches you back. Every reach reshapes the one who reaches ([[Casting & Corruption|Corruption]] → [[Mutation]]). Push too far and you become **untethered** — your mind dissolves into the loom, and what's left wears the shape of a demon, hunted by everyone who survives you.

## Weavers vs. "wizards"

The world has wizards and witches and spellbooks. **They are not weavers.** They are scavengers, using relics and anomalies that real weavers left behind ([[Magic Items]], [[Wild Magic|wild-magic scars]]).

> [!quote] The secret truth
> **Weavers are the secret, true source of all magic — and they are a dying species, because the power that makes them eats them alive.**

## The one impossible thing

The Weave will not grant a **wish.** The Weave *is* the wish that made everything — it cannot wish upon itself.

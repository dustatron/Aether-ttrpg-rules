# Becoming an Aetherborn

No one is born an Aetherborn. Every Aetherborn was once an ordinary person, awakened by another Aetherborn, or true caster.

## Awakening

Any Aetherborn with at least **6 current Exposure** can awaken another person by transferring that Exposure to them.

1. The existing Aetherborn transfers 6 Exposure to the recipient. Subtract 6 from the existing Aetherborn’s current Exposure.
2. The recipient becomes an Aetherborn and begins with 6 Exposure.

Awakening gives the recipient the permanent ability to perceive and command Aether. It does not give the true caster control over the person they awaken.

A magical disaster, Wild Magic, or accidental contact with raw Aether cannot awaken someone on its own. Another true caster must awaken them.

Every Aetherborn has someone who awakened them, and that person was awakened by someone else. The chain stretches back through generations. How the first Aetherborn came to be remains a mystery.

## Who awakened your character?

During [[Character Creation]], consider:

- Who awakened your character?
- Why did they give the character access to Aether?
- What has the character done with that power since Awakening?

Every new Aetherborn begins with 6 Exposure received during Awakening. The player still chooses whether their character starts Unchanged or Marked. See [[Character Creation#Step 4: Awakening and starting magic|starting rules]] for Mutations and Spell Tags.

Every Aetherborn is a source of new magic, a possible creator of relics, and a possible future Demon. The magical orders have reasons to find them first.

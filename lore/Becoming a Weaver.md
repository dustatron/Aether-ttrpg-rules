# Becoming a Weaver

No one is born a weaver. There are two ways the [[The Weave|Weave]] gets into a person.

## Initiation — deliberate, and never free

A weaver can intentionally open a normal person to the Weave. This is not charity — it is a **transaction.** In exchange for access to power, the new weaver pays their maker by handing over some of their own **life** (in the rules-lite build, this is fictional debt; see design note below). The flow of life runs **upward, from initiate to maker.**

This is how a powerful weaver cheats their own doom — converting people and taxing them to extend a life that [[Mutation|mutation]] is burning down. The price is whatever they can demand. **The desperate pay more.**

A made weaver is born **in debt,** inside a structure, beneath someone. See [[The Church & The Prophet]].

> [!question] Design note — rules hook
> In the archived Mana build, initiation transferred **Injury slots.** Rules-lite has no Injury slots — it uses [[Death & Injury|Hearts + Wounds]]. Decide the mechanical price of initiation (a Wound? a Heart cap? purely fictional debt?) before it matters at the table.

## Exposure — accidental, and free

Survive raw contact with weaving and the Weave takes root in you anyway. Two triggers:

- **Survive lethal weaving** aimed at you and live.
- **Survive a [[Wild Magic|wild magic event]].**

No one chose to make you. You owe no one. A **wild weaver** is unindebted, unaffiliated — and hunted by both the world *and* the covens, who see free vitality walking around unclaimed.

## Every weaver is a contagion

> [!warning] Violence and carelessness manufacture the very thing the world hunts
> Use lethal magic and fail to finish the job → you have made a weaver. Roll double 1s in a crowd ([[Wild Magic]]) → the survivors may wake to the Weave. Each new weaver is a beacon that may point back at you. This is why weavers practice restraint.

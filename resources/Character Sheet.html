Casting isn't a stat. Your cast bonus = number of mutations + a spell tag that fits. A fresh weaver casts at +1 in their signature style, +0 in anything else.

Corruption

A successful raw cast adds 1 (casting through an item is free). Fill all 5 → mutate, then clear. Spend corruption to forge items & reshape the world.

Hope

Gain 1 whenever the Hope die is higher (hit or miss). Spend to push a roll from no to yes, or bank all 5 to raise a stat.

Body & Death

Hearts — your health. Fill = lose a life, then reset (heal between fights)
Armor
Armor rating
Blocks any hit of equal or lower harm. A hit greater than your armor gets through — reduce armor by 1 instead of taking the harm. Repair or replace it later.
Wounds — your lives. Each filled Heart-track costs one.
Wounds until death
Default 3 — the GM sets this at campaign start (the deadliness dial). Fill it and your spirit separates from your body.

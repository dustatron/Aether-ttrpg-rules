# AI Context

This vault contains the current rules for **Aetherweave RPG** (working title, previously **The Arcane Aether**) and archived drafts from earlier versions.

## Authority Order

When sources disagree, use this order:

1. The user's latest instruction.
2. The topic notes linked from [[Rules/index|Core Rules]], [[Character Creation]], [[Monsters]], and linked v4.5 notes marked `canonical: true`.
3. [[Friend Feedback Checklist]] for mechanics that are not settled.
4. Lore and brainstorm notes.
5. `archive/` only when researching history. Archived rules are never current authority.

## Current Terminology

Use **Aetherweave RPG** as the working game title, **Aether** for the substance of reality, and **Aetherborn** or **true caster** for someone capable of **True Casting**.

Every Aetherborn must be awakened by another Aetherborn. See [[Becoming an Aetherborn]]. An Aetherborn with at least 6 current Exposure can transfer 6 to another person to permanently awaken them. Ritual, contact, and timing requirements remain undecided. New characters start with 6 Exposure received during Awakening. Keep the current relic rules, Fatigue, inventory, and final-slot Demon transformation. The six-Mutation ending and magnitude-priced starting relics in the November 14 update were rejected.

The Aetherweave is the hidden current of freeform magic beneath reality. Other traditions of magic can coexist within it. Existing filenames, system identifiers, and PDF labels may retain The Arcane Aether.

Use **Health** for the damage track. **Harm** is no longer the name of that resource.

The earlier names **The Resonance**, **Resonance**, and **Attuner** appear in archived material. Do not restore them to current notes.

Use **player** for the real person at the table. Use **character** for the person in the game world.

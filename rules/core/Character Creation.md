# Character Creation: Making a Weaver

## At a glance

1. [[#Step 1: The Basics|The Basics]] (name, age, pronouns, description)
2. [[#Step 2: Life Type|Life Type]] (one trait)
3. [[#Step 3: Archetype|Archetype]] (array, gear, perk)
4. [[#Step 4: Set Your Stats|Set Your Stats]] (+2 / +1 / 0 / −1)
5. [[#Step 5: Goals|Goals]] (what you seek)
6. [[#Step 6: Flaw or Fear|Flaw or Fear]] (lean in, gain Hope)
7. [[#Step 7: Backstory / Core Concept|Backstory]] (core concept)
8. [[#Step 8: Exposure|Exposure]] (first spell)
9. [[#Step 9: Starting Values|Starting Values]] (0 Corruption · 5 Hearts · 0 Hope · 0 Wounds · 1 tag)

## Step 1: The Basics
- **Name**: Provide a name you will like
- **Age**: What age do you want your character to be
- **Pronouns**: What gender or lack of would you like your character to have.  
- **Description**: Quickly describe how they look.

## Step 2: Life Type
Life type represents a fantasy inspired creature or humanoid being you want your character to be. 

| Life Type     | Trait                                                                                                             |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Human**     |                                                                                                                   |
| **Elf**       |                                                                                                                   |
| **Goblin**    | *Overlooked.* +1 to hide, squeeze, scurry, or go unnoticed.                                                       |
| **Beastfolk** | *Clawed & keen.* Natural weapons (1 harm) and sharp senses. +1 to notice by scent or sound, or to climb and leap. |

## Step 3: Archetype

Your characters Archetype is intended to be an inspirational starting point for your stats  and gear. A way to lean into tropes and get starting playing right away. 

> [!example] Fighter: *lived by strength and steel*
> - **Starting Stats:** Force +2, Grace +1, Wits 0, Heart −1
> - Gear: Longsword (2 harm), throwing hand-axe (1 harm), wooden shield (armor 1), chainmail (armor 1), a war-horn that carries for miles.
> - *Hard to Put Down* (perk): the first time you'd lose a life in a fight, stay up with 1 Heart instead.

> [!example] Thief: *lived by wit, speed, and other people's things*
> - Array: Grace +2, Wits +1, Heart 0, Force −1
> - Gear: Double daggers (2 harm), leather armor (armor 1), lockpick kit, grappling hook, 60' rope, smoke pellets (drop one to vanish from sight).
> - *Slip Away* (perk): once per day, escape a grab, a corner, or a closing door without a roll.

> [!example] Scholar: *lived by books, signs, and things best left unread*
> - Array: Wits +2, Heart +1, Grace 0, Force −1
> - Gear: Quarterstaff (1 harm), spyglass, 3 reference books, a vial of poison, a magic stone that always returns to your hand.
> - *I've Read About This* (perk): once per day, recall a small piece of trivial knowledge about something or someone in the scene.

> [!example] Charmer: *lived by fast talk and knowing everyone worth knowing*
> - Array: Heart +2, Grace +1, Wits 0, Force −1
> - Gear: Hidden dagger (1 harm), padded coat (armor 1), disguise kit, marked cards and loaded dice, a ring that lets you recall any face you've seen.
> - *Everyone Knows Me* (perk): once per day, you turn out to have history with someone present.

## Step 4: Set Your Stats

Arrange +2, +1, 0, −1 across your four stats:

- **Force:** strength, violence, endurance.
- **Grace:** speed, stealth, precision.
- **Wits:** perception, knowledge, cunning.
- **Heart:** charm, will, presence. The Weave erodes this as you [[Mutation|mutate]]; scar tags hit Heart hardest.

Everyone has one thing they're bad at. The −1 is a feature.

## Step 5: Goals

What does this character want? Name one or two things: a person to find, a debt to pay, a wrong to right. These give you reasons to act, and reasons to keep [[Casting & Corruption|casting]] even as it damns you.

## Step 6: Flaw or Fear

Name one flaw or fear in a sentence. For example: *terrified of deep water · can't back down from a dare · lies when cornered · won't abandon anyone.*

> [!tip] Play it, gain Hope
> When you lean into your flaw or fear, letting it drive a choice or complicate a moment, mark 1 [[Core Rules\|Hope]].

## Step 7: Backstory / Core Concept

A sentence or two tying it together: who you were, what shaped you. Just enough to play.

## Step 8: Exposure

Only those who've touched the raw Weave can ask it for favors. How were you exposed? (See [[Becoming a Weaver]].)

- You were caught in another weaver's magic, and it soaked into you instead of killing you.
- You survived a [[Wild Magic|wild magic event]] and walked out changed.
- A weaver poured it into you on purpose, and you may owe them for it.

*(Easy group option: one event exposed you all. Same disaster, same survivors, an instant bond and a built-in mystery.)*

Your first spell: as the Weave took hold, something broke out of you. Name it. This is your starting [[Spell Tags|spell tag]] (*Flame, The Drowned, Rot, Binding…*). You didn't choose the style; it surfaced when the Weave changed you.

## Step 9: Starting Values

| Value | Start |
|---|---|
| [[Casting & Corruption\|Corruption]] | 0 |
| [[Death & Injury\|Hearts]] | 5 |
| [[Core Rules\|Hope]] | 0 |
| [[Death & Injury\|Wounds]] | 0 *(death at 3 by default; GM sets)* |
| [[Mutation\|Mutations]] | 0 |
| [[Spell Tags]] | your one first spell |
| [[Casting & Corruption\|Casting bonus]] | mutations + a fitting tag → +1 in your first-spell style, +0 otherwise |

> [!note] Casting is not a stat you buy
> Force, Grace, Wits, and Heart are for the mundane world. The Weave is its own thing, powered by what it's turning you into. Magic grows only by [[Mutation|mutating]] (+1 each) and by gaining more [[Spell Tags|tags]].

You start weak and unproven. The rest you discover in play.

# Core Rules

> [!abstract] In one line
> Everything is **2d6 + stat (or casting bonus)** against a **difficulty** the GM sets for the scene. Two differently-colored dice — one **Hope**, one **Fear** — say two things at once: *did it work,* and *what did it cost.*

Every action — fighting, sneaking, persuading, noticing, **and** [[Casting & Corruption|casting]] — uses the same roll.

## The dice

Roll **2d6**. Use two colors: one **Hope** die, one **Fear** die.

**2d6 + the relevant stat** (or, for casting, your [[Casting & Corruption|casting bonus]]) **vs. a difficulty target:**

| Difficulty | Target |
|---|---|
| **Easy** — routine | **6** |
| **Medium** — real effort *(the default)* | **8** |
| **Hard** — a serious challenge | **10** |
| **Very Hard** — few succeed | **12** |
| **Legendary** — beyond skill | **14** *(see below)* |

**Meet or beat the target = success. Under it = failure.**

## Reading the two dice

The higher-colored die colors the outcome:

| | **Hope die higher** | **Fear die higher** |
|---|---|---|
| **Success** | **Yes** — clean. Gain 1 Hope. | **Yes, but** — it works, with a complication. GM gains 1 Fear token. |
| **Failure** | **No, but** — you miss, with a small consolation. Gain 1 Hope. | **No, and** — you miss, and it gets worse. GM gains 1 Fear token. |

> [!tip] Partial success = "yes, but"
> A success rolled with **Fear high** is your bargain: you got what you wanted, and it cost something.

On a **tie** between the dice, the player takes the **Hope**.

## The two overrides

- **Doubles (2–6)** → **critical success.** "Yes, and" — you succeed and something extra happens.
- **Double 1s** → **critical fail.** "No, and" — you fail and something bad happens.

Casting adds more to these — see [[Casting & Corruption]] and [[Spell Tags]].

## Legendary actions (target 14)

Legendary requires the Weave's attention:

- You can only **attempt** a Legendary weave with **at least 1 [[Mutation]]** — the Weave won't listen to the still-pristine.
- On a Legendary attempt, the **only** crit is **double 6s** (auto-triumph). Other doubles don't crit at this tier; resolve them against the 14.

> [!note] Why Legendary sits so high
> With only their stats, a normal person basically can't reach 14. A weaver whose casting bonus has grown through mutations and stacked spell tags *can*. The Weave is how you punch past human limits.

## Hope & Fear — the two currencies

Every roll hands one currency to somebody: **Hope** to the player on a high Hope die, a **Fear token** to the GM on a high Fear die.

### Hope — the player's luck and growth

Gain **1 Hope** whenever the **Hope die is higher** — on a success *or* a miss. **Cap 5.**

Spend Hope to:

- **Push a roll** — turn a *no* into a *yes*, or upgrade an outcome one step.
- **Grow** — bank it; **5 Hope raises a stat** (or earns a new move, in downtime).

You also gain **1 Hope** when you lean into your character's **flaw or fear** in play (see [[Character Creation]]).

> [!tip] Hope is the reward for boldness
> You gain it most by rolling, risking, and reaching. Spend it now to save yourself, or hold it to become more. In a game about slow doom, spending your last Hope means something.

### The Fear token — the GM's currency

When the **Fear die is higher**, the **GM banks a Fear token.** Fear is a **single, table-wide pool** filled by *every* player's rolls. **Cap 10.** The GM spends tokens to introduce complications, bring in threats and hunters, make [[Running the Game#Hard Move|hard moves]], **power enemy weavers' casting**, or make the Weave itself convulse. Full spend list: [[Running the Game#Spending Fear tokens]].

> [!note] Why Fear caps higher than Hope
> [[#Hope — the player's luck and growth|Hope]] is **per player** (cap 5); Fear is **one shared pool** the GM fills from the whole table. Capping it at **2× Hope** gives a table's worth of Fear room to bank without constant overflow.

> [!danger] The engine of play-to-find-out
> The players' own risky magic *fuels the danger they face.* Nobody — not even the GM — knows what the Fear tokens will buy.

## What applies to which roll

| | Every roll | Casting only |
|---|:---:|:---:|
| Hope/Fear die (gain Hope / bank Fear) | ✅ | ✅ |
| [[Casting & Corruption\|Corruption]] (+1 on a successful raw weave) | | ✅ |
| Double 6s → [[Spell Tags\|spell tag]] | | ✅ |
| Double 1s → [[Wild Magic]] | | ✅ |

On mundane rolls, doubles and snake-eyes still crit up or down — just without tags or wild magic.

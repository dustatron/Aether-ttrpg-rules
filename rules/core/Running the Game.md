# Running the Game

> [!abstract] The stance
> Set a situation, then **play to find out.** The GM doesn't plan the story — the dice and the players' choices reveal it.

## Play to find out

The [[Core Rules|Fear tokens]] surprise everyone. The [[Casting & Corruption|Corruption]] creeps. The [[Mutation|mutations]] arrive unbidden. Nobody at the table knows how a weaver's story ends until it does — in monstrousness, in death, or in the quiet horror of surviving.

## GM principles

- **Set scale by fiction, not by list.** A candle's flame may need no roll. A wall of fire is a weave. Unmaking a city is a weave with a price you name *out loud, up front,* before the player commits.
- **Reward the brave; let the cowards be safe and small.** Item-hoarders who never [[Mutation|mutate]] are *meant* to be weak. Don't punish them with rules — punish them with the fiction. They are scavengers, not weavers.
- **This is a short arc by design.** A character realistically takes **3–4 mutations** across a campaign before reaching the horizon. The game is the brief, incandescent life of a weaver. Tell the table that going in.
- **The players' magic fuels the danger.** Every high Fear die banks you a [[Core Rules|Fear token]]. Spend them to make the world push back.

## Spending Fear tokens

Fear is a **single, table-wide pool** filled by every player's high-Fear rolls. **Cap 10** (twice the per-player [[Core Rules#Hope — the player's luck and growth|Hope]] cap of 5). Spend it to push the world back:

| Fear spend | Cost |
|---|---|
| Move or spotlight an enemy in battle | **1** |
| Introduce a complication, twist, or cost | **1** |
| **Power an enemy weaver's cast** | **scale to the weave** — 1 (small) → 3+ (legendary) |
| Make a **hard move** against the players | **2–3** |
| Bring in a fresh threat or hunter | **2–3** |
| Make the Weave itself convulse ([[Wild Magic]]) | **GM's call** |

> [!note] The villain-side mirror
> Enemy weavers spend **Fear** to cast, the way PCs spend nothing but risk [[Casting & Corruption|Corruption]]. It keeps the opposition's magic on the same throttle as the players'. More Fear banked = more (and bigger) enemy casting.

### Hard Move

A move that causes harm, or otherwise shifts the thrust of the current adventure's narrative — not a small complication but a real turn of the screw.

> [!tip] Costs are a starting dial
> These numbers are a draft to feel out in play, not law. Adjust once you've seen how fast Fear actually banks at your table.

# Character Creation v2: Making a Caster

Make someone who had a life before the Resonance got in. Start with a job, a grudge, or someone they care about.

## Step 1: Who the character is

Name the character and choose their life type. Add whatever interests you: age, looks, voice, where they are from. A line is plenty. The rest can come out in play.

### Life type

Humans, elves, goblins, cat people, karate turtles. There is room for strange people in this world. Choose a life type and record its trait.

| Life type | Trait |
| --- | --- |
| Human | **Stubborn.** Add +1 to a roll when the character endures, holds on, or refuses to break. |
| Elf | **Long-lived.** Add +1 when the character's old knowledge, old languages, or an old grudge matters. They were there. |
| Goblin | **Overlooked.** Add +1 when the character hides, squeezes, scurries, or goes unnoticed. |
| Beastfolk | Animal-like people, such as cat people or karate turtles. Their natural weapons deal 1 damage. Their additional trait needs a design pass; see the draft notes below. |
| Otherfolk | Vampires, oozes, lizard people, or something the table hasn't met before. Choose one body advantage that fits the character. |

Otherfolk is a wide door. Maybe the character is a crab person, a large intelligent ooze, or someone with antlers. Say what they are, then choose one advantage: natural weapons, a shell or thick hide, keen senses, climbing, swimming, or short flights.

Natural weapons deal **1 damage**. A shell or thick hide gives **1 armor**. For the other advantages, add **+1 to a roll when the chosen advantage matters**. Describe how the character uses it. A turtle takes the shell. A cat takes the senses. A crab takes the claws.

> [!question] Life traits still need a pass
> Beastfolk's old trait used Grace and Wit, which are not stats in v4. It needs a replacement. We also need to decide whether a life trait can raise a roll above +3 or apply to casting.

## Step 2: Choose an Archetype

Choose an archetype. Take its gear, maximum Harm, and one perk. Use its suggested stats, or arrange **+2, +1, 0, and −1** across the four stats yourself.

| Stat | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| Strength | Muscle, force, fighting hand to hand |
| Dexterity | Speed, stealth, precision, ranged attacks |
| Willpower | Endurance and resolve |
| Charm | Persuasion and presence |

Set the character's **current Harm equal to their maximum Harm**. See [[Core Rules v4#Harm, scars, and recovery|Harm, scars, and recovery]].

### Archetypes

| Archetype | The character's old life | Maximum Harm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[Hunter]] | Tracked Attuners for coin, then woke up as one. | 7 |
| [[Thief]] | Lived by wit, speed, and other people's things. | 6 |
| [[Charmer]] | Lived by fast talk and knowing everyone worth knowing. | 6 |
| [[Monk]] | Studied the ancient arts and honed their body into a weapon. | 7 |
| [[Fighter]] | Someone paid them to stand in the way, and they never learned to stop. | 8 |
| [[Witch]] | Had a place in an order once, then something answered. | 5 |

| Archetype | Strength | Dexterity | Willpower | Charm |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hunter | +2 | 0 | +1 | −1 |
| Thief | −1 | +2 | +1 | 0 |
| Charmer | −1 | +1 | 0 | +2 |
| Monk | +1 | +2 | 0 | −1 |
| Fighter | +2 | +1 | 0 | −1 |
| Witch | −1 | 0 | +2 | +1 |

Pick one perk from the linked archetype and record what it does. Apply any starting stat increase, to a maximum of +3 for that stat.

> [!note] The perk tables are still being written
> Choose a written perk that works with v4. Some rows are blank, “advantage” is undefined, and the Monk's ration-free recovery perk no longer works with the current recovery rules.

If none of the archetypes fit, talk with the GM about the character's old life and use the same stat array. Agree on their starting gear, one perk, and maximum Harm using the existing archetypes as references. Record those choices before play.

### Pack the gear

The character has **12 inventory slots**. Equipment fills from the top down. Their starting mutation will take slot 12, leaving eleven slots for gear and any later conditions.

Start with these basics, then add the archetype's gear:

- **Firelight:** a torch, flint and steel. One slot.
- **Rations:** three. One slot.

If the archetype already lists a torch or three rations, those are the same starting supplies. Don't add a second set.

An item usually takes one slot. Small things of the same kind bundle together: a handful of arrows, spikes, or doses. Worn armor does not use a slot. Record weapons, their damage, and the character's armor separately so they are easy to find during a fight. See [[Core Rules v4#Damage and armor|damage and armor]].

You may swap gear if the table agrees it fits the character's old life, but everything must fit in the pack.

Conditions fill from the bottom up and can displace gear. See [[Core Rules v4#Inventory and conditions|inventory and conditions]].

## Step 3: Key goals

Write up to four clear goals for your character and agree on them with the table. Serious or ridiculous both count.

> [!example] A character's key goals might be
> - [ ] Get revenge on the person who killed their mother.
> - [ ] Burn down the silly hat shop.
> - [ ] Find out what happened to their sister.
> - [ ] Buy back the family house.

**Earn 2 [[Core Rules v4#Luck Points|Luck]] when your character achieves one of their key goals and you cross it off their list.**

Tell the other players. Their characters may want to help, or have a very good reason to keep that hat shop standing.

## Step 4: How the Resonance got in

Decide how the characters touched the Resonance, what changed, and why they remain together.

Every character starts with **one mutation**. Roll on the [[mutation-tables|d66 mutation table]] and take what comes. It is the mark of the day the Resonance got in. Write the mutation in slot 12.

Choose **one +1 spell tag** connected to the mutation or the character's first magic. Fire, healing, floating, talking to animals, or something else that fits. Agree on its scope with the GM. It adds +1 when it fits a casting roll, but never reduces Exposure or limits what the character can attempt.

The starting mutation lets the character safely handle **4 Exposure in one cast**. See [[Core Rules v4#Mutations and casting limits|mutations and casting limits]].

### Before the first scene

Check the sheet together:

- Name, life type, and trait.
- Four stats, an archetype or agreed background, and one perk.
- Current and maximum Harm.
- Gear in its slots, weapon damage, and armor.
- Up to four key goals.
- One mutation in slot 12 and one +1 spell tag.
- Current Exposure, Luck, and a reason to stay with the crew.

> [!question] Starting tracks
> Working proposal: start with **0 Luck and 0 current Exposure**, with no scars or fatigue. The first mutation records what already happened to the character. Earlier creation rules did not explicitly set these starting tracks.

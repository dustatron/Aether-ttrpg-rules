# Monk

Studied the ancient arts and honed their body into a weapon.

**Stats:** Strength +1, Dexterity +2, Willpower 0, Charm -1

**Maximum Harm:** 7

## Gear

- Walking staff, 2 damage
- Bandages and salves, 3 uses, restore 1d6 Harm
- 60 feet of knotted rope
- Throwing stars
- Torch
- 3 rations

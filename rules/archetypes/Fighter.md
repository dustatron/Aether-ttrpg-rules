# Fighter

> [!abstract] Someone paid you to stand in the way, and you never learned to stop

**Stats:** Strength +2 · Dexterity +1 · Willpower 0 · Charm −1
**Harm:** 8

**Gear** 
- Longsword (2 damage)
- Chainmail (armor 1)
- Shield (armor 1)
- Torch
- 3 rations

## Perks

**Roll a d6, or pick one.**

| d6  | Perk                                                    |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Add your **Strength** to damage in melee                |
| 2   | Once a fight, take a hit meant for someone at **Close** |
| 3   |                                                         |
| 4   | Drop an enemy and you may move one zone at once         |
| 5   |                                                         |
| 6   | +1 to any one stat, to a maximum of +3                  |

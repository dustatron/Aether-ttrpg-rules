# Thief

> [!abstract] Lived by wit, speed, and other people's things

**Stats:** Strength −1 · Dexterity +2 · Willpower +1 · Charm 0
**Harm:** 6
**Gear** 
- Double daggers (2 damage)
- Leather armor (armor 1)
- Lockpick kit
- Grappling hook
- 60' of rope
- Smoke pellets (drop one and vanish from sight)
- 3 rations

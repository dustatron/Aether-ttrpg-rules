# Charmer

> [!abstract] Lived by fast talk and knowing everyone worth knowing

**Stats:** Strength −1 · Dexterity +1 · Willpower 0 · Charm +2
**Harm:** 6

**Gear**
- Short sword (2 damage)
- Disguise kit
- A ring of stolen keys
- Padded coat (armor 1)
- Torch
- 3 rations

## Perks

**Roll a d6, or pick one.**

| d6  | Perk                                                                  |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Once a day, you know a guy who can help                               |
| 2   | Once a day, give advantage to any roll                                |
| 3   | Strangers assume you belong here until you give them a reason         |
| 4   | Once a session, name someone who owes you a favour. They do           |
| 5   | You can lie to someone who already knows better, and be believed once |
| 6   | +1 to any one stat, to a maximum of +3                                |

# Witch

> [!abstract] You had a place in an order once, and then you were excommunicated 

**Stats:** Strength −1 · Dexterity 0 · Willpower +2 · Charm +1 
**Harm:** 5

**Gear** 
- A Staff from your old order, 3 charges of an effect. (You did not make)
- Sleep powder
- A bone-handled knife (1 damage)
- torch
- 3 rations

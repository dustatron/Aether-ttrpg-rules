# Witch

Had a place in an order once, then something answered.

**Stats:** Strength -1, Dexterity 0, Willpower +2, Charm +1

**Maximum Harm:** 5

## Gear

- A staff from the character's old order, holding 3 Exposure and an effect they did not create
- Sleep powder
- A bone-handled knife, 1 damage
- Torch
- 3 rations

## Perks

Roll 1d6 or pick one.

| d6 | Perk |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Increase one Stat by 1, to a maximum of +3. |
| 2 | Gain or improve a Spell Tag when the kept casting dice are double 4s or double 5s. |
| 3 | Increase Charm by 1, to a maximum of +3. |
| 4 | Your character can befriend as many ordinary cats as they wish. The cats bite on their behalf. |
| 5 | Roll 3d6 and keep the highest two when casting. |
| 6 | Your character can gather a murder of crows that obeys them. |

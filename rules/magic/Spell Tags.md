# Spell Tags — What You're Good At

A spell tag is a magic style you've become good at through practice — *Flame, The Drowned, Rot, Binding, Whispering Glass.*

> [!tip] When your weave fits the tag, add +1 to your casting roll.
> Tags **stack** — different tags that all fit one cast add together.

## How you get them

- **Start with one** — your signature, the magic that came to you in your inciting incident.
- **Mutate** — each [[Mutation]] lets you name a new tag.
- **Roll double 6s** on a cast — gain a tag with no mutation.

## Deepening a tag

Roll **double 6s** in a style you *already* have a tag for and it **deepens** instead of adding a new one: that tag climbs to **+2**, then **+3**, and so on.

> [!note] The specialist's reward
> Casting the same magic again and again is how a specialist blows past what a generalist can reach. A deep tag stacks on top of your mutation-driven casting bonus.

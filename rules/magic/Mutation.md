# Mutation — What Corruption Makes You

Each mutation triggers at **every 5 [[Casting & Corruption|Corruption]]** and is a permanent step. When it hits:

1. **+1 to your casting bonus.** The Weave answers more readily as you become less human. *(This is the only way [[Casting & Corruption|Casting]] gets stronger.)*
2. **Name a new [[Spell Tags|spell tag]]** for the magic you've been working — **+1** whenever you weave that style.
3. Gain a permanent **mutation** — a physical change and a **scar tag**. Roll your level's [[mutation-tables|table]], or decide what fits the story.

## The mutation ladder

| # | The change |
|---|---|
| 1 | A mark — glowing eyes, small claws, sharpened teeth. Hideable under a hood. |
| 2 | A deformity — a limb warps; clothing hides it, barely. |
| 3 | A transformation — a new limb, a tail, a mouth that shouldn't be. |
| 4 | A hunger or a new sense — visible on the body, with a need that comes with it. |
| 5 | The mask slips — you no longer pass as human. The hunters know you. |
| 6 | **MONSTER** — you are lost to the Weave. The character becomes an NPC. |

Detailed spark tables per level: [[mutation-tables|Mutation Tables]].

## Scar tags

> [!warning] The downside of every mutation
> When the fiction works against your monstrousness — hiding, being trusted, moving quietly, passing as human — take **−1.** Scar tags erode **Heart** rolls generally (see [[Character Creation]]).

A relevant scar tag cancels a relevant [[Spell Tags|spell tag]] on the same roll.

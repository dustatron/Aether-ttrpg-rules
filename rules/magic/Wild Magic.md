# Wild Magic — When the Loom Convulses

> [!danger] Trigger
> **Double 1s on a [[Casting & Corruption|cast]]** → critical fail *and* the Weave erupts. Roll on the wild magic table.

On mundane rolls, double 1s is just a bad miss ("no, and"). Only **casting** triggers wild magic.

## Severity

> [!question] Open design question — NOT FINALIZED
> Severity needs a rules-lite hook. The [[archive/the-weave-playbook#WILD MAGIC — When the Loom Convulses|archived Mana build]] scaled severity by the caster's Mana pool — but rules-lite has no Mana pool. Candidate hooks: current [[Casting & Corruption|Corruption]], number of [[Mutation|mutations]], or GM [[Core Rules|Fear tokens]] spent. **Pick one before play.**

## The table

The wild magic table itself is still a **brainstorm** — an unsorted pile of ideas, not yet a rollable table.

> See [[wild-magic-table]] in `tables/` — silly and harmless at the low end, escalating to reality-warping at the high end.

> [!warning] Wild magic almost always changes the physical space forever
> Every old weaver battlefield is a zone of broken physics — which is exactly where the world's fake magicians find their relics. See [[The Church & The Prophet]].

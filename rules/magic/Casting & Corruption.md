# Casting & Corruption

> [!abstract] One mechanic, two faces
> Casting is how you bend the world. **Corruption is what casting costs** — and it is *both your fuel and your ruin.* You cannot separate the act from the mark it leaves.

---

## The WEAVE move

> [!quote] The move
> **When you reach into the Weave to force a change on the world,** describe exactly what you want, and roll **2d6 + your casting bonus + spell tag (if any)** against the [[Core Rules|difficulty]].

There is **no spell list.** If you can picture it and the GM agrees the Weave could do it, attempt it — steal from any myth or story. The GM sets [[Core Rules|difficulty]] by scale: a candle may be Easy or need no roll; a wall of fire is Medium; unmaking a city is Legendary, priced up front.

> [!warning] The one forbidden thing
> The Weave will not grant a **wish.** The Weave *is* the wish that made everything — it cannot wish upon itself.

## Your casting bonus

Casting is **not a stat you buy.** Your casting bonus is:

> **number of [[Mutation|mutations]] + any [[Spell Tags|spell tag]] that fits the weave**

A fresh weaver casts at **+1 in their signature style** (their one starting tag) and **+0 in anything else** — shaky, wild, dangerous. Magic grows *only* by mutating (+1 each) and gaining more tags.

---

## Corruption — power and doom in one number

> [!danger] A successful raw cast gives you +1 Corruption
> The Weave listened — so it marked you. Failed casts don't corrupt; the fabric simply ignored you.

- You **gain** Corruption by successfully casting raw (**+1** per success).
- You **spend** it to forge [[Magic Items]] and to work permanent changes on the world.
- **Every 5 Corruption you don't spend, you [[Mutation|MUTATE]].**

Five mutations. The **6th crossing makes you a monster** — untethered, lost, gone.

### The furnace

You are a furnace: doom pours in every time your magic works, and your only relief is to forge that doom into objects before it tips you over.

> [!danger] Stop creating, and you transform.
> Raw casting fills you with Corruption. Forging [[Magic Items|items]] bleeds it back out into the world. Every cast is a choice — take the mark, or cast safely through an item you bled to make.

## Casting without Corruption

Two ways to weave and take no mark:

- **Through a [[Magic Items|magic item]]** — spends one of the item's charges.
- **Trivial effects** the GM rules need no roll and no cost.

---

## Critical results (casting only)

- **Double 6s** → gain a [[Spell Tags|spell tag]] with **no mutation** — or, if a tag already fits, it **deepens** instead (+2, then +3…).
- **Double 1s** → roll on the [[Wild Magic]] table.

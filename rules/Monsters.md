# Monsters

> [!abstract] Five numbers and two words
> **Threat, damage, Harm, armor, morale**, then **what undoes it** and **what it wants**. That fits on an index card.

## The tiers

Do not build monsters. Choose one.

| Tier             | Threat | Damage | Harm | Armor | What it is                  |
| ---------------- | ------ | ------ | ---- | ----- | --------------------------- |
| **Mook**         | −1     | 1      | 3    | 0     | A goblin                    |
| **Baddy**        | +0     | 2      | 6    | 1     | A bandit, a soldier, a bear |
| **Glass cannon** | +1     | 3      | 3    | 0     | A mummy                     |
| **Monster**      | +1     | 3      | 15   | 2     | An ooze                     |
| **Horror**       | +2     | 4      | 20   | 3     | A demon                     |
| **Legendary**    | +3     | 5      | 30   | 5     | A dragon, the Prophet       |

## Running one

- **It rolls 2d6 + Threat.** See [[Core Rules v4#When enemies attack|when enemies attack]].
- **A crowd rolls once,** at the best single weapon among them. See [[Core Rules v4#When enemies attack|when enemies attack]].
- **Name its attacks. All of them deal the one number.** A bear bites and swipes. Both are 2.
- **Weakness.** The lever that beats grinding it down. Only a Legendary must be ended through its weakness.
- **Desire.** What it wants besides your death. Anything with a desire can be bargained with.
- **Morale.** At or below **half its Harm**, roll **2d6 + Threat**: [[Core Rules v4#Morale and Fleeing|morale and fleeing]].
- **Plant a way to learn every weakness:** a corpse, a book, a survivor, a [[Hunter]] who has seen one.

**Seven worked entries:** [[Bestiary]].

## Legendary

> [!danger] Harm alone never kills a Legendary
> Fill all 30 and it does not die. **Only its weakness ends it,** and finding that weakness is the campaign.

**Grind it down and choose.** It **withdraws**, unhurt and coming back, or it **changes**, back to full and worse.

**Telegraph it.** From the first hit, describe the wound closing and the blade coming away clean.

**It is rarely where you think.** Killing the robe in the room kills only the robe.

**It has no weak spot.** Weapon and spell damage can lower its Harm, but a critical still cannot end it. **The weakness is not a shortcut. It is the only door.**

**It still checks morale,** and that is what "grind it down" means. Reaching half its 30 Harm buys you the roll, not the kill.

## Morale

**At or below half its Harm, roll for morale.** The GM may call for another roll after it takes more damage: [[Core Rules v4#Morale and Fleeing|morale and fleeing]].

| Tier | Harm | Mark |
|---|---|---|
| **Mook** | 3 | 1 |
| **Baddy** | 6 | 3 |
| **Glass cannon** | 3 | 1 |
| **Monster** | 15 | 7 |
| **Horror** | 20 | 10 |
| **Legendary** | 30 | 15 |

**An entry may set its own mark.** Half Harm is the default, not the rule. A mark equal to its Harm means it checks the first time it is hurt at all.

**Threat is the morale bonus.** A Mook at −1 breaks more often than not. A Legendary at +3 breaks one time in twelve, and when it does it withdraws rather than dies.

> [!warning] Some things never check
> **Mindless, driven, or bound.** An Ooze is only hungry. A Mummy is punishing whoever opened the door. A bound relic-servant cannot choose. Write **never** on the entry and skip the roll.

**A `7 to 9` is what the Desire is for.** It stops fighting you and starts pursuing what it actually wants.

## Enemy Attuners

The most common Horror in this world is a person who went too far.

**Pick a tier for the body. Pick a mutation count.** Roll them on the [[mutation-tables|d66 table]] and do not choose. What you roll **is** the description.

**Mutations set its ceiling,** exactly as they set a player's: [[Core Rules v4#Mutations and casting limits|mutations and casting limits]].

**Its weakness is always its own Exposure.** Make it cast.

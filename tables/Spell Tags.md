# Spell Tags

> [!note] Not a rule. A prompt sheet.
> An Arcanist has **no spell list**. The player describes what they want their character's magic to do and accepts its Exposure cost. See [[Core Rules#Magical casting|magical casting]].
>
> This list exists for the moment a player goes blank. Skim it, take an idea, cast the idea. Nothing here is permission, a cost, or automatically a tag on the character's sheet.

## Physical

- **Alter** - Change the properties of an existing thing.
- **Create** - Bring something into existence.
- **Destroy** - Break down or erase something.
- **Transform** - Turn one thing into another.
- **Move** - Push, pull, lift, or relocate something.
- **Shape** - Manipulate physical form.
- **Bind** - Restrain or hold something in place.
- **Separate** - Divide or pull things apart.
- **Repair** - Restore something damaged.
- **Enhance** - Strengthen or improve something.

## Life

- **Heal** - Restore physical injury.
- **Harm** - Damage a living thing.
- **Animate** - Give movement to something.
- **Resurrect** - Restore life.
- **Mutate** - Alter a living form.
- **Sustain** - Preserve life or prevent deterioration.
- **Drain** - Remove vitality from something.

## Mind

- **Sense** - Perceive something normally hidden.
- **Reveal** - Expose hidden information or things.
- **Conceal** - Hide something from perception.
- **Influence** - Alter thoughts, emotions, or behavior.
- **Communicate** - Transmit thoughts or information.
- **Remember** - Access, alter, or restore memories.
- **Forget** - Remove or obscure memories.

## Space and time

- **Teleport** - Move instantly across space.
- **Reach** - Interact with something at a distance.
- **Anchor** - Prevent movement or displacement.
- **Distort** - Alter space.
- **Accelerate** - Make something happen faster.
- **Slow** - Make something happen slower.
- **Stop** - Temporarily halt an action or process.
- **Loop** - Repeat a moment or action.

## Energy and elements

- **Fire**
- **Cold**
- **Lightning**
- **Force**
- **Light**
- **Darkness**
- **Sound**
- **Wind**
- **Water**
- **Earth**
- **Gravity**

## Reality

- **Illusion** - Create a false perception.
- **Ward** - Protect against an effect.
- **Dispel** - Undo another magical effect.
- **Nullify** - Suppress an effect or property.
- **Transmute** - Change one substance into another.
- **Duplicate** - Create a copy.
- **Erase** - Remove something from reality.
- **Manifest** - Make an idea or concept physically real.
- **Probability** - Alter the likelihood of an outcome.
- **Causality** - Alter the relationship between cause and effect.

## Aether

- **Attune** - Establish a connection with something.
- **Resonate** - Amplify an existing pattern.
- **Desynchronize** - Disrupt a pattern.
- **Echo** - Reproduce an existing effect.
- **Imprint** - Leave a magical pattern in an object.
- **Unmake** - Break down a pattern.
- **Rewrite** - Fundamentally alter a pattern.
- **Harmonize** - Bring two patterns into alignment.
- **Fracture** - Split or destabilize a pattern.
- **Anchor** - Lock a pattern in place.

# THE WEAVE — Mutation Tables
### Spark tables for mutation events.

> [!abstract] Status: usable spark tables
> System-neutral — works with the active [[Mutation]] rules. Roll or pick. These are **sparks, not law.**

---
## Level 1 — The Mark
*Something is visibly wrong, but small enough to hide under a hood, a glove, a scarf, a closed mouth.*

| #   | Mutation                                                               | Scar tag       |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------- |
| 1   | Your eyes glow — faintly in daylight, unmistakably in the dark.        | *Glowing Eyes* |
| 2   | Your fingernails harden and curve into small claws.                    | *Clawed*       |
| 3   | Your teeth sharpen to points.                                          | *Sharp Teeth*  |
| 4   | Gill-slits open along your neck or ribs.                               | *Gilled*       |
| 5   | Your ears grow long and pointed, pushing back against your skull.      | *Pointed Ears* |
| 6   | Patches of scale or fur creep across the backs of your hands and neck. | *Marked Hide*  |

---

## Level 2 — The Deformity
*A limb or feature warps. Clothing hides it — barely.*

| d6 | Mutation | Scar tag |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | One hand elongates — too many knuckles, fingers too long. | *Wrong Hand* |
| 2 | Patches of scale, bark, or chitin spread across your skin. | *Hide* |
| 3 | Your teeth sharpen and multiply; your smile is a threat now. | *Maw* |
| 4 | One leg bends the wrong way, like a beast's hind leg. | *Beast-legged* |
| 5 | Your spine curves and juts; you stand hunched, taller, wrong. | *Hunched* |
| 6 | Your nails become claws that won't retract or cut away. | *Clawed* |

---

## Level 3 — The Transformation
*No hiding it up close. You are visibly not just a person anymore.*

| d6 | Mutation | Scar tag |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A new limb pushes free — a third arm, a grasping tail. | *Extra Limb* |
| 2 | Wings unfurl from your back. They don't fly. Not yet. | *Winged* |
| 3 | A second mouth opens somewhere it shouldn't — throat, palm, chest. | *Second Mouth* |
| 4 | Your lower face splits into mandibles or a muzzle. | *Wrong Face* |
| 5 | Your flesh turns translucent in places; things move beneath. | *Glass Flesh* |
| 6 | Horns, antlers, or spines erupt from your skull and shoulders. | *Crowned* |

---

## Level 4 — The Hunger / The Sense
*You gain something — and a need that comes with it.*

| d6 | Mutation | Scar tag |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Huge black night-eyes — you see in full dark, but daylight makes you flinch and squint. | *Nightbound* |
| 2 | A gaunt, sunken hunger sets into your face and frame; you crave something wrong (heat, blood, fear) and visibly wither without it. | *The Hunger* |
| 3 | Slitted, flaring nostrils or a forked tongue that tastes the air — you scent emotion and intent, and it shows. | *Bloodhound* |
| 4 | Your chest goes still — you don't breathe, and the unnatural stillness is plain to anyone watching. | *The Stillness* |
| 5 | Feelers, whiskers, or quivering membrane-frills sprout to drink in sound and vibration; you hear everything. | *All-Hearing* |
| 6 | Your hands blacken and chill; your touch visibly withers what it holds too long. | *Withering Touch* |

---

## Level 5 — The Mask Slips
*You cannot pass as human at a glance. Crowds flee. The hunters know your name.*

| d6 | Mutation | Scar tag |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Your lower half is no longer flesh — coils, roots, smoke, or many legs. | *Monstrous Form* |
| 2 | You loom — half again as tall, wrong-proportioned, impossible to mistake. | *Towering* |
| 3 | Your face is gone, remade into something with no human features left. | *Faceless* |
| 4 | Light bends around you; you are hard to look at directly, and wrong when seen. | *Unlookable* |
| 5 | Your body is a chorus of the earlier mutations, layered, nothing human left whole. | *Abomination* |
| 6 | You flicker between forms, never quite solid, never quite here. | *Unfixed* |

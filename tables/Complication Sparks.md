# Complication Sparks

The character succeeds, but something makes the situation more complicated. Scan for a spark that fits what just happened, then describe the problem it suggests. Keep the success, as described in [[Core Mechanics#The dice]].

These are prompts for improvising, not conditions or fixed mechanical penalties. The columns suggest different levels of impact. Choose what fits the scene.

| Mild | Moderate | Severe | Extreme |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Noticed | Watched | Followed | Identified |
| Scuffed | Damaged | Broken | Destroyed |
| Delayed | Diverted | Cut off | Trapped |
| Doubt | Suspicion | Distrust | Hostility |
| Extra favor | Uneven deal | Debt | Lasting obligation |
| Clue left | Trail left | Witness | Secret exposed |
| Rival alerted | Rival arrives | Rival interferes | Rival gains power |
| Off balance | Exposed | Separated | Surrounded |
| Time lost | Opportunity lost | Deadline nears | Crisis arrives |
| Odd trace | Unwanted noise | Lingering effect | Spillover |

For example, the character opens the vault, but leaves a trail. The vault is open. Someone now has a way to follow them.

## Costs

| Spark | Possible cost |
| --- | --- |
| Spend | Coin or supplies |
| Lose | Gear or time |
| Owe | A favor |
| Suffer | Damage |

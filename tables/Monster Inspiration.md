# Monster Inspiration

Choose or roll each column separately. Make it fit the creature.

| d6 | Weakness | Desire |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Fire / heat | Hot gossip |
| 2 | Cold / ice | A stolen egg returned |
| 3 | Light / sunlight | A safe home |
| 4 | Sound / vibration | A new body |
| 5 | A vulnerable body part, such as an eye, a joint, or a gap in its armor. | An admiring audience |
| 6 | A source of strength that can be cut off, such as contact with the earth, a relic, or a ritual. | An old debt paid |

Make the weakness suggest something the characters can try: freeze the creature, expose it to daylight, reach the gap in its armor, or separate it from its source of strength. Decide how that changes the situation.

Plant a clue to the weakness through the creature's behavior, its surroundings, or a story about it. The desire offers a bargain.

Use [[Monsters#Tiers|the monster tier table]] for stats, [[Monsters#Running one|Running one]] for Threat, and [[Monsters#Morale|Morale]] when the creature might flee, surrender, or bargain.

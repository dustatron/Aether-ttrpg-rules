# WILD MAGIC EVENTS — Brainstorm
### The double-1s table for *The Weave*

> [!warning] Status: BRAINSTORM — not a finished table
> An unsorted pile of ideas, not yet rollable. The [[Wild Magic|rules-lite Wild Magic]] mechanic still needs a **severity hook** (the old severity note below assumes the archived **Mana** pool, which rules-lite doesn't have). Sort and finalize before play.

*Working scratchpad. No structure yet — just collecting ideas. The plan: silly and harmless at the low end, escalating to genuinely dangerous and reality-warping at the high end.*

---

## The Pile (unsorted)

**Yours:**
- People nearby are turned into dogs.
- Gravity in the area drops to that of the moon.
- Everything in the area turns to sand.
- A sudden windstorm kicks up.
- A crazy light show fills the sky.
- People nearby grow antlers.

**Riffing in the same silly key:**
- Every nearby small object becomes faintly, harmlessly sticky.
- The caster's shadow detaches and starts doing its own thing (mostly mimicking, occasionally rude).
- Gravity stays normal but *down* is now whichever way the caster is facing.
- All liquids within sight turn to jelly.
- A choir of disembodied voices hums dramatic music that follows the scene around.
- Every animal in the area becomes briefly, alarmingly articulate.
- Color drains from the world — everything greyscale — for a while.
- It begins to rain upward.
- Everyone's hair grows three feet instantly.
- The nearest inanimate object becomes sentient and deeply anxious about it.
- Everyone nearby swaps voices with the person to their left.

**Added:**
- The area is plunged into total darkness for **1d6 months.**
- Everyone nearby is shrunk to the size of a mouse.
- For a brief moment, everyone in the near area glimpses the **entire universe** — its full size, its full age — and feels, in a real and bodily way, how small and unimportant they are in the grand scheme of things. (Some laugh. Some never recover.)

**Dangerous (high end — reality breaking):**
- A tear opens in the air and something on the *other side* notices. It does not come through yet. But it knows the caster's face now, and it is patient.
- Everyone within reach ages forward **2d10 years** in an instant — bones, memory, grief and all. (The caster too.)
- The ground becomes a mouth. The earth in the area is briefly, hungrily alive and tries to swallow what stands on it.
- All fire within sight goes out — every flame, every spark, every warmth — and will not catch again in this place. The cold that follows is not natural.
- A second, wrong sun rises and hangs in the sky for a day. Everything its light touches withers; shadows point the wrong way; nothing grows here again for a generation.
- The caster's own death is briefly made true *somewhere* — they collapse, stop, are gone for a held breath — then snap back, having felt every second of it. (GM: this may cost an Injury, or worse, leave a mark.)
- Blood — everyone's, all at once — tries to leave the body. A held, horrible moment where staying alive is a contest of will.
- The boundary between the living and the dead thins here. The recently dead in the area sit up. They are not friendly, and they remember dying.
- Time fractures: everyone present relives the **last ten seconds** on a loop, aware, unable to stop, until the Weave releases them (1d6 loops). What they do differently each time may stick.
- A pillar of raw, unshaped Weave erupts from the caster — a detonation that injures everyone nearby and **exposes survivors to the Weave** (new weavers are born here), leaving a permanent scar in the world.
- Everyone nearby, including the caster, **mutates once.** No save, no choice. The Weave reshapes the flesh of all who witnessed it.
- Names come undone. For a time, no one here can remember who anyone is — including themselves. Some never get the name back.
- Reality forgets this place exists. For 1d6 days the area cannot be entered or left, found on any map, or remembered clearly by those outside it. Then it returns — changed.

## Notes to self
- Keep the low end *fun to roll* — the kind of result the table laughs at.
- Somewhere in the middle, the silly ones should start having teeth (moon gravity is funny until someone's mid-swordfight; dogs is funny until one of them was holding the only key).
- High end = the permanent reality scars: gravity broken *forever*, light dies *forever*, detonations, mass mutation.
- The dangerous ones split into flavors: **bodily** (aging, blood, forced mutation), **existential/mind** (universe glimpse, forgotten names, lost identity), **the dead** (corpses rise, death thins), **the watched** (something on the other side notices), and **the scar** (place erased, wrong sun, detonation). Could pick one of each band for variety when we finalize.

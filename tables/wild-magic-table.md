# Wild Magic

When a casting roll is double 1s, roll **2d6 and add the character's current Exposure**. Then apply the result.

| Total | Wild Magic |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | A wild light show fills the sky. |
| 3 | Every small object nearby becomes faintly sticky. |
| 4 | Everyone nearby grows three feet of hair. |
| 5 | Every animal nearby can speak for the rest of the scene. |
| 6 | The character's shadow detaches and starts acting on its own. |
| 7 | All liquids nearby turn to jelly. |
| 8 | Everyone nearby swaps voices with the person to their left. |
| 9 | People nearby grow antlers. |
| 10 | Rain falls upward for 1d6 minutes. |
| 11 | People nearby are turned into dogs for the rest of the scene. |
| 12 | Gravity in the area drops to that of the moon for the rest of the scene. |
| 13 | Down becomes whichever direction the character is facing for the rest of the scene. |
| 14 | Everyone nearby shrinks to the size of a mouse for 1d6 hours. |
| 15 | Everything nearby that is not alive turns to sand. |
| 16 | The area is plunged into total darkness for 1d6 months. |
| 17 | A tear opens in the air. Something on the other side sees the character and remembers them. |
| 18 | Everyone nearby ages 2d10 years. |
| 19 | Time repeats the last ten seconds 1d6 times. Everyone remembers each loop. |
| 20 | A pillar of raw Aether erupts from the character. Everyone nearby is hurt and exposed. Survivors may become Arcanists. |
| 21 | Everyone nearby, including the character, Mutates once. |

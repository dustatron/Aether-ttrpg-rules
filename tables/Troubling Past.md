# Troubling Past

Some ideas for [[Character Creation]]. Choose one or roll **d66**: roll two six-sided dice, with one giving the tens digit and the other the ones. Decide which is which before rolling. A 3 and a 5 gives 35, not 8.

| d66 | Troubling past                                                                                                                                                                              |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 11  | Your character's family died in one winter. The neighbors divided the house before the ground thawed.                                                                                       |
| 12  | Your character's child went missing, and the town decided your character was to blame and excommunicated them.                                                                              |
| 13  | Your character was exiled for a murder they did not commit.                                                                                                                                 |
| 14  | Your character lost the farm, the name, and the dog to a debt they inherited.                                                                                                               |

| 22  | Your character's mother sold them to settle a debt. She cried, and then she counted the coins.                                                                                              |
| 23  | Your character's village paid the raiders to leave, and the payment was them. They came back years later to find the village had kept the money.                                            |
| 24  | Your character's younger sibling died of a fever while your character was away earning the money for the healer.                                                                            |
